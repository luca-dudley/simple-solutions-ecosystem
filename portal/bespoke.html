Technical Consultancy FAQ

Questions About Custom Builds

Do you build outside of ClickUp? +

Absolutely. ClickUp is a versatile starting point, but we engineer custom solutions choosing the right tool for the job — Microsoft Power BI, Supabase Postgres backends, Make.com pipelines, serial hardware bridges, and custom offline TypeScript Progressive Web Apps.

Do workflow builds incur third-party software subscription costs? +

Yes. Simple Solutions charges a fixed project fee for custom architecture, automation engineering, and team onboarding. Any third-party software subscriptions required for your pipeline (such as ClickUp, Microsoft Power BI, Make.com, or cloud databases) are paid directly by your organization to ensure 100% data ownership and direct licensing control.

How does fixed-scope project pricing work? +

After the 30-minute discovery call, we define a clear, milestone-based scope with an unalterable fixed price for each deliverable milestone. There are zero surprise hourly charges or open-ended consulting bills.

Who owns the intellectual property and data at handover? +

You do — completely. At live handover you receive full ownership of your data, dashboards, custom automations, scripts, and workspace administrative credentials.
