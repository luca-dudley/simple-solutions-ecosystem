Operational FAQ

Straight answers, no sales fog.

Why do you price per operating site instead of per user seat? +

Per-seat billing discourages managers from giving operational software to frontline labor, drivers, and operators[cite: 1, 3]. With site pricing, you can deploy the tools across your entire workforce without paying extra per person[cite: 1, 3].

Do general workers need email accounts to sign off on tasks? +

No. Only managers and supervisors have authenticated login profiles[cite: 3, 4]. Field workers are held on your internal worker directory and attributed to actions via supervisor verification or physical QR badge scans[cite: 3, 4].

How does offline sync work in remote orchard blocks and warehouses? +

Our tools run as offline-first Progressive Web Apps with local IndexedDB storage[cite: 1, 4]. You can complete inspections or spray logs with zero connectivity; the data syncs automatically and idempotently once you reconnect[cite: 1, 4].

Can you build a custom ClickUp workspace or AI pipeline for our business? +

Yes. Through our Custom Systems division, we deliver fixed-scope ClickUp workspace architectures, executive Power BI dashboards, and automated AI data extraction pipelines with 100% client data ownership[cite: 5, 6].
