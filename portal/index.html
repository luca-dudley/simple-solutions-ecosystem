Operational FAQ

Straight answers, no sales fog.

Why do you price per site instead of per user seat? +

Agriculture and industry run on large frontline teams. Charging per user punishes businesses for digitising their entire workforce.

We charge per operating site, which means every supervisor, seasonal worker, operator, and manager can use the platform without increasing your subscription costs.

Do general workers need email addresses or user accounts? +

No.

Frontline workers can be linked to training records, task sign-offs, and operational activities through QR badges or supervisor verification. Only managers and administrators need platform logins.

Will the system work without internet? +

Yes.

The Ecosystem is built for real-world conditions where connectivity is unreliable. Workers can continue capturing information in the field, orchard, warehouse, workshop, or processing facility, and records synchronise automatically when a connection becomes available.

Can I start with just one module? +

Absolutely.

Whether you need compliance training, spray traceability, procurement workflows, or packhouse intake management, each module can be deployed independently and expanded as your requirements grow.

What support is included? +

Every subscription includes onboarding assistance, product support, and platform updates.

We're a South African team building software for South African operations. When you need help, you speak to people who understand your environment, not a call centre on another continent.

Can the platform be customised for my operation? +

Yes

Every business works differently. Beyond our standard modules, we build custom portals, dashboards, integrations, automations, hardware bridges, and operational workflows tailored to your environment.
