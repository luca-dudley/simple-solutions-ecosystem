Operational FAQ

Straight answers, no sales fog.

Why do you price per operating site instead of per user seat? +

Traditional SaaS per-seat pricing penalizes agriculture and heavy industry because estates employ hundreds of seasonal laborers. Pricing per seat creates a perverse incentive to exclude frontline staff from digital records. With our flat site pricing, you can deploy the tools across your entire workforce without paying extra per person.

Do general workers need email accounts to sign off on tasks? +

No. Only control-plane managers and supervisors hold authenticated login profiles. Frontline workers exist on your tenant worker registry and are attributed to tasks via privacy-preserving QR badges or supervisor verification.

How does offline sync work in remote orchard blocks and warehouses? +

Our tools operate as offline-first Progressive Web Apps with client-side IndexedDB databases (Dexie.js). High-priority transactional records submit instantly via an outbox over 2G/EDGE networks upon reconnection. Photos and heavy files queue separately in the background.

Do you engineer custom software or ClickUp systems outside standard tools? +

Yes. Through our Bespoke Systems & Data Architectures division, we build custom ClickUp operational workspaces, Power BI executive telemetry dashboards, hardware scale bridges, and automated AI data extraction pipelines with 100% client intellectual property ownership.
